Never use these "AI slop" design patterns:
- Cyan/purple glow effects on dark backgrounds
- Dot-grid or generic geometric backgrounds
- Neon accent colors (#00d4ff, #00ff88, #ff3355)
- Inter, Roboto, system fonts
- Purple-indigo gradients

**Why:** Paul specifically called out that these look obviously AI-generated. "nhìn vào biết ngay" — you can tell at a glance it's AI.

**How to apply:** Always start with warm, muted, or unconventional color palettes. Use textures like grain/noise instead of geometric patterns. No glow effects. Think premium engineering tool, editorial design, or vintage aesthetic — never sci-fi/gaming dashboard.
